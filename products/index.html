BOOM

The operating system for trading & distribution businesses.

What it is

BOOM runs the full commercial cycle in one system — orders, inventory, purchases, suppliers, customers, payments, outstanding dues and live profit. One source of truth, on web and mobile, so the office and the field are looking at the same numbers at the same time.

It came out of a distribution business run on spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads, where nobody could answer “what did we actually make this month” without a week of reconciliation. BOOM is the answer to that, built into a product.

Ownership model

BOOM is licensed, not rented indefinitely. One-time license, self-hosted on your server, no per-seat fees. Support and updates are things you choose to keep, not conditions of keeping the lights on.

Built for

  • Distribution & wholesale
  • Trading businesses
  • Multi-branch & multi-warehouse operations
  • FMCG and consumer goods supply
  • Field sales and delivery-led teams
  • Manufacturing with an outbound sales arm

What changes

  • Live profit per order, per customer, per product
  • Stock that reflects reality, not last week’s count
  • Dues and collections visible before they become a problem
  • The whole team in one system instead of five chat groups

Scrollwalk

Listing pages buyers walk through — from one phone video.

What it is

Scrollwalk is listing software for real estate agents, teams and owners selling their own home. You film one walkthrough of a home on your phone. Scrollwalk turns it into a scroll-controlled walkthrough on a branded listing page — with AI-drafted copy, captions, photos, floor plans, enquiries, viewing requests and WhatsApp, all under your name.

Buyers move through the home by scrolling: slowly to linger, quickly to move on, back up to look again. No 3D camera. No app for buyers. One link for Instagram, WhatsApp and the signboard.

Delivery model

Scrollwalk is SaaS. Agents publish into a shared platform, so listings stay live, process video and collect enquiries without each agent hosting their own stack. Plans count active listings; sold homes stay up free as a portfolio. Pre-launch access opens 23 October 2026.

Built for

  • Solo real estate agents
  • Teams and agencies under one brand
  • Owners selling privately (one-off listing)
  • Phone-first buyers on WhatsApp and Instagram
  • India (RERA fields) and USD markets first

What changes

  • A Reel becomes a page that can take the enquiry
  • No special 3D or 360° camera required
  • Views turn into forms, viewings and WhatsApp leads
  • One link that works in bios, chats and QR codes
