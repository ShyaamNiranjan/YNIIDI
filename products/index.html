What is BOOM?

BOOM is YNIIDI’s business operations platform — self-hosted, customizable, and sold on a one-time license. It puts orders, inventory, purchases, suppliers, customers, billing, payments and workflows into one system on your own infrastructure, so nothing depends on a spreadsheet being up to date or a chat thread being read.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is BOOM self-hosted?

Yes. BOOM is designed for self-hosted deployment on your infrastructure, giving you control over data and environment.

Can BOOM be customized for our workflows?

Yes. BOOM is fully customizable — modules, workflows, and interfaces can be tailored to your operations.

Does YNIIDI only build BOOM?

BOOM is our lead product and the right starting point for operations work. Custom builds are the second path — websites, web apps, integrations and BOOM extensions — taken on when BOOM isn’t the fit.

How do I start a custom project?

Contact us at services@yniidi.com or use the contact form. Describe your requirements and we'll respond with next steps.
