The Belief

Most companies don’t own their operations. They lease them.

The systems that hold your orders, your margins, your customers and your history are usually rented — billed per seat, per month, forever. Stop paying and the business stops working. That isn’t infrastructure. That’s a tenancy.

  • Rent never ends. The invoice scales with your headcount, not with the value you get.
  • Your data sits somewhere else. Export is a favour, not a right.
  • The software bends you. You reshape how you work to fit a tool built for someone else.
  • Leverage moves away from you. Pricing, roadmap and shutdown dates are decided without you in the room.

We think the software running a business should be an asset on your side of the table. That principle holds whether we license you a product or build you a system from scratch.
