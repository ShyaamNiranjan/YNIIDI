What is YNIIDI?

YNIIDI is a technology company founded by Shyaam Niranjan. We build our own software products — starting with BOOM, a self-hosted business operations platform — and we also build custom solutions for clients: websites, web applications, and bespoke software across any field or industry.

The Problem

Businesses Need Software That Fits — Not Forces Change

Off-the-shelf tools rarely match how a business actually operates. Teams end up juggling spreadsheets, disconnected apps, and workflows that don't scale.

YNIIDI exists to solve that — with products you can host yourself and customize, and custom builds designed around your requirements.

  • Fragmented systems and manual workarounds
  • Software that can't adapt to your processes
  • No ownership over your data or deployment
  • Generic solutions that ignore your industry
