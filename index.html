What is YNIIDI?

YNIIDI is a technology company founded by Shyaam Niranjan. We build our own software products — starting with BOOM, a self-hosted business operations platform sold on a one-time license — and we take on custom builds when a business needs something outside BOOM’s scope.

The Problem

Most Businesses Outgrow Spreadsheets — Then Start Renting The Replacement

Orders arrive in chat, stock lives in a file someone updates when they get time, and supplier dues sit in one person’s head. The usual fix is a subscription that moves all of that onto someone else’s servers, priced per user, forever.

YNIIDI builds the other option: systems you host, customize and keep — paid for once.

  • Orders, stock and dues tracked in three places that never agree
  • Software that can’t bend to how your team actually works
  • No control over your own data or deployment
  • Costs that grow every time you add a person
