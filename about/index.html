About YNIIDI

Built From Watching A Real Business Drown In Spreadsheets

YNIIDI builds operations software companies own and run themselves. BOOM came out of a manufacturing operation on Excel and WhatsApp — outdated stock counts, supplier dues held in memory, orders that sometimes went missing. It was built module by module against that business.

Our position: your operations shouldn’t be rented.

Who is YNIIDI?

YNIIDI (You Name It, I’ll Do It) is a technology company founded by Shyaam Niranjan. We build BOOM, a self-hosted business operations platform sold on a one-time license, and we take on custom builds — web applications, internal tools and integrations — when a business needs something BOOM isn’t meant to do.

Vision

Systems Companies Own Outright

The software a business runs on shouldn’t be something it can lose access to. We build for self-hosting and one-time ownership: your data on your servers, your workflows in your hands, no renewal that can be raised or withdrawn.

Over time, more products in the same shape — practical, operational, and yours after you pay for them.

How We Build

Built Against Real Operations

Every module in BOOM exists because a real operation needed it — a stock count that was wrong, a supplier balance nobody could confirm, a task that fell between two people. We add capability when it removes a specific failure, not because it sounds modern.

Self-Hosted Architecture Workflow Automation Operational Reporting Custom Integrations Data Migration From Excel Role-Based Access

I watched a business run its entire operation on spreadsheets and WhatsApp — and lose orders, stock and margin to it every week. The tools on offer wanted a subscription and its data.

So we built BOOM: one system, on their servers, paid for once. When BOOM isn’t the right answer, we build what is.

Shyaam Niranjan Founder, YNIIDI
