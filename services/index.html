Companies choose a custom build for one of two reasons: their process is their edge and no product respects it, or the tools they’re renting have quietly become the ceiling on how they operate. Both are good reasons.

What We Build

Your app. Your workflows. Just for you.

Custom Software Development

Systems built around the process that already makes your business work — operations platforms, internal tools, and the software that runs the parts nobody else does the same way. We start from how your team actually operates, not from a feature checklist.

SaaS Product Development

Your product idea taken from scope to a working platform — multi-tenant architecture, billing, roles, admin and the operational plumbing that makes it sellable. We build it as a business asset, not a demo that stalls at the first real customer.

Mobile Applications

Android and iOS apps for teams that work away from a desk — field sales, delivery, service, inventory on the floor. Designed for one-handed use in real conditions, and built to stay usable when the connection isn’t.

Web Applications

Dashboards, customer portals, ordering systems and internal platforms that hold up under daily load. Fast to move through, clear about what matters, and structured so your team gets the number they need without asking someone for it.

Websites & Brand Sites

Sites that carry the weight of a serious company — considered design, real performance, and copy that says something. Built on foundations your team can update, with the search and speed fundamentals handled properly rather than bolted on later.

System Integrations & Automation

We connect the systems you already run — accounting, ERP, e-commerce, logistics, payments, messaging — so data moves once instead of being re-entered three times. The manual reconciliation work stops, and the errors that came with it stop too.

Product Extensions & Custom Modules

Running a licensed YNIIDI product and need it to do something specific to your operation? We extend it — custom modules, bespoke reporting, additional workflows and integrations built onto your deployment. You get the proven core plus the part only you need.

Technology Consulting & Scoping

Before anything gets built, someone has to decide what should be. We map the operation, define the system, size the work and tell you plainly what to build, what to buy and what to leave alone — including when the answer is that you don’t need us yet.

How Engagements Work

Scoped work. Defined deliverables. You own the result.

No retainers that drift, no billing by the hour into an undefined outcome. Every engagement has a shape agreed before it starts.

Scoped projects

We define the system, the boundaries and the timeline up front. If something falls outside scope, we say so and price it separately rather than absorbing it silently.

Clear deliverables

You know exactly what lands — screens, modules, integrations, environments and documentation. Progress is reviewable throughout, not revealed at the end.

Full ownership on handover

Source code, infrastructure and data are yours. You can run it, extend it, or hand it to another team entirely. Nothing about the handover is designed to keep you dependent.

Support by choice

Most clients keep us on for maintenance and future phases. That’s a decision they make each time — not a condition of the software continuing to work.
